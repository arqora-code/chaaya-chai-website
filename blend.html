Kadak Masala

Our house chai. Malty Halmari Assam, green cardamom pounded that morning, Kochi ginger and a pinch of Malabar pepper.

₹ 780
1
Weight
100 g, about 50 cups
Leaf
Halmari estate CTC, second flush
Spice
Cardamom, ginger, black pepper, clove
Brew
1 tsp per cup · boil 3 min · milk to taste

Brew it in clay if you can.

Our kulhads are thrown by the Prajapati family and fired in a wood kiln. Unglazed, they drink a little of the chai back and return it as the smell of first rain. Every tin of Kadak ships with two.

How to brew it

A letter from the neem tree, once a month. Harvest notes, new blends, nothing else.
