Chai room

Three and a half minutes that hold a city together

01

Leaf, water, spice

One spoon of Kadak per cup into cold water. Bring it up slowly; the cardamom opens at the first bubble.

02

Milk from height

Pour it from a hand's height so it folds through the brew. Watch it turn from tar to copper.

03

Pull it twice

Between the degchi and the kulhad, through the air. The froth is the point.

04

Rest it in clay

The unglazed kulhad drinks a little back and gives the chai its earth. Drink, then return the cup to the soil.

Brew it at home tonight.
